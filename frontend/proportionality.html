Proportionality Levels

Not all investigations require the same rigor. Match your methodology to the stakes, deadlines, and consequences of your work. Smart investigators know when to go deep and when to move fast.

"Rigor is not an end in itself, but a means to serve investigative objectives."
⚡ Why Proportionality Matters

The Problem: Most OSINT practitioners either apply maximum rigor to everything (wasting time and resources) or cut corners everywhere (producing unreliable intelligence). Both approaches fail.

The Solution: Match your methodology to the actual stakes. A 20-minute fact-check doesn't need forensic documentation. A court case absolutely does. Think of it like medicine: you don't hospitalize someone for a headache, but you don't treat a heart attack with aspirin.

The Pareto Principle: 80% of your investigations can be handled with Quick methods. 15% need Standard rigor. Only 5% require Full Rigor. Save your energy for what truly matters.
