The Five Pillars

These five pillars define the objective of a structured OSINT methodology. Each explains why rigorous practice is essential: to guarantee reproducibility, highlight uncertainties, counter cognitive biases, preserve ethical standards, and ensure operational relevance. Understanding them transforms routine inquiries into actionable and reliable intelligence, demonstrating that methodology is not bureaucracy, but the structural foundation of rigorous analysis.

The falsifiability

Established by Karl Popper, learn to structure your conclusions so that they can be tested and refuted, and never again rely on intuition alone.
