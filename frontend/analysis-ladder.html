Analysis Ladder

Transform raw data into actionable intelligence through structured progression. Each step filters noise, validates information, and builds understanding until you reach genuine insights.

"You cannot reach level N+1 without solidly establishing level N"
🎯 Why the Analysis Ladder?

The Problem: Most analysts jump directly from raw data to conclusions, skipping critical validation steps. This creates unreliable intelligence full of cognitive biases and unverified assumptions.

The Solution: The Analysis Ladder forces systematic progression. You climb one step at a time: collect data, validate identifiers, understand context, detect patterns, then synthesize insights. Each level filters out noise and adds value.

The Result: By the time you reach the top, you've eliminated 99% of the noise. What remains is pure, actionable intelligence with calibrated confidence levels. No guesswork—just rigorous methodology.

🪜 The 5 Levels
5
Insights
Actionable intelligence with calibrated confidence. Predictions, recommendations, and decisions backed by the full analytical chain.
4
Patterns
Recurring behaviors and anomalies. Temporal trends, linguistic signatures, coordination indicators, and statistical regularities.
3
Contextual Data
Relationships and environment. Social graphs, geographic context, temporal baseline, and community mapping.
2
Identifiers
Verified anchor points. Emails, usernames, IPs, phone numbers—each confirmed by 2+ independent sources.
1
Raw Data
Unverified collection. Screenshots, profiles, posts, documents—everything gathered before validation begins.
⚠️ Critical Rules
🚫 Never skip levels. Each step validates the one below. Jumping from raw data to insights produces unreliable conclusions. Document any forced jumps as risks.
✅ Validate everything at Level 2. An identifier needs confirmation from 2+ independent sources. One source = speculation. Two sources = verified anchor point.
📊 Quantify patterns at Level 4. "Often" isn't good enough. Use actual numbers: frequency, standard deviation, sample size (n≥10 minimum).
🎯 Calibrate confidence at Level 5. Every conclusion needs a confidence level (Low/Medium/High/Very High) with explicit justification. Never claim certainty.
📝 Document limitations always. What don't you know? What assumptions did you make? What could prove you wrong? Honest uncertainty beats false confidence.

Level 1 Raw Data Collection
Collected profile screenshot, last 50 tweets, follower list (first 100), profile images, external links shared. Total: 156 raw items.
  • No conclusions yet—just gathering everything visible
  • Documented: timestamps, hashes, URLs
    • Level 2 Identifier Validation
    Found email in bio, confirmed on GitHub and breach database. Username matches Reddit account. Timezone UTC+3 confirmed via metadata.
  • Email: verified (3 sources) ✓
  • Username: verified (2 sources) ✓
  • Location hint: verified (metadata consistent) ✓
    • Level 3 Context Mapping
    Mapped social graph: primarily follows crypto accounts (68%). Active in #Bitcoin and #DeFi communities. Posts mostly 9am-6pm UTC+3 (likely work hours).
  • Community: Crypto/blockchain focused
  • Baseline: 3-5 posts/day, weekdays only
  • Network: 12 mutual connections with known traders
    • Level 4 Pattern Detection
    Detected: Posts concentrated 10am-12pm and 2pm-4pm (lunch break pattern). Uses specific phrases repeatedly. Reacts to market movements within 30 minutes.
  • Temporal pattern: 85% posts in work hours (n=120 tweets)
  • Reactivity: Fast response to news (avg: 18 minutes)
  • ANOMALY: Sudden 20 tweets at 3am (likely event-driven)
    • Level 5 Actionable Insight
    Conclusion: Professional crypto trader based in Eastern Europe, active during market hours. Legitimate account with moderate OPSEC.
  • Confidence: High (78%) - multiple sources confirm
  • Prediction: Will post 10am-4pm UTC+3 weekdays (80% probability)
  • Limitation: Real identity not confirmed, location approximate
  • Recommendation: Monitor during market volatility for insights
